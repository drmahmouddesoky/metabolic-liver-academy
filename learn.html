The basics 6

The basics

What is fatty liver (MASLD)?

Fatty liver means extra fat is stored inside the liver cells. When it is linked to weight, blood sugar, blood pressure or blood fats, doctors call it MASLD.

1 min read
The basics

Why did the name change from NAFLD to MASLD?

In 2023, liver societies changed the name to describe the real cause, metabolic problems, and to remove words that felt judgemental.

1 min read
The basics

What is MASH, and how is it different?

MASH is the active, inflamed form of MASLD. The fat is now harming liver cells, and over years this can cause scarring.

1 min read
The basics

How common is fatty liver?

Very common. About 1 in 3 adults worldwide has fatty liver. In the Middle East it is closer to 4 in 10, and about 7 in 10 people with type 2 diabetes.

1 min read
The basics

What causes fatty liver?

Mostly insulin resistance: the body struggles to handle sugar and fat, so the liver stores the extra as fat.

1 min read
The basics

Is fatty liver serious?

For most people it stays mild. But in some it leads to scarring, cirrhosis or liver cancer, and it raises the risk of heart disease in everyone.

1 min read

Symptoms and tests 6

Symptoms and tests

Does fatty liver cause symptoms?

Usually not. Many people feel completely well, even when there is scarring.

1 min read
Symptoms and tests

My ultrasound says "fatty liver". What now?

Don't panic and don't ignore it. The next step is to find out if there is scarring, starting with a simple blood-test score called FIB-4.

1 min read
Symptoms and tests

What are liver enzymes (ALT and AST)?

ALT and AST are proteins that leak into the blood when liver cells are irritated. They are clues, not a full answer.

1 min read
Symptoms and tests

What is the FIB-4 score?

FIB-4 is a free score calculated from your age and three routine blood results. It tells your doctor whether serious scarring is unlikely or needs a closer look.

1 min read
Symptoms and tests

What is a FibroScan, and how do I prepare?

FibroScan is a quick, painless scan that measures how stiff your liver is. Stiffer means more scarring.

1 min read
Symptoms and tests

Do I need a liver biopsy?

Usually not. Today most people are assessed with blood-test scores and scans. A biopsy is kept for unclear cases.

1 min read

Daily life 7

Daily life

How much weight do I need to lose?

Even 5% of your body weight helps. Losing 7–10% can calm inflammation, and 10% or more can improve scarring.

1 min read
Daily life

What should I eat?

A Mediterranean-style diet works best: lots of vegetables, legumes, whole grains, fish and olive oil, with fewer sugary and processed foods.

1 min read
Daily life

Which drinks are good or bad for my liver?

Water is best. Plain coffee may even help the liver. Sugary drinks and packaged juices are among the worst.

1 min read
Daily life

What exercise helps fatty liver?

Any activity you can keep doing. Aim for 150 minutes a week of brisk walking or similar, plus some strength exercise.

1 min read
Daily life

Can I fast in Ramadan with fatty liver?

Most people with fatty liver can fast safely. Check with your doctor first if you have diabetes, cirrhosis or take several medicines.

1 min read
Daily life

Is alcohol safe with fatty liver?

No amount is proven safe. Alcohol adds to the damage from fat, so less is better and none is best if there is scarring.

1 min read
Daily life

Do herbs, "detox" or supplements help?

No herb, detox or supplement is proven to treat fatty liver. Some herbal products can even harm the liver.

1 min read

Treatment 3

Treatment

Is there a medicine for fatty liver?

Yes, for some people. Two medicines are now approved in several countries for MASH with moderate or advanced scarring. They work alongside lifestyle change, not instead of it.

1 min read
Treatment

Are statins (cholesterol pills) safe for my liver?

Yes. Statins are safe in fatty liver and protect your heart. Do not stop them because of fatty liver or mildly raised enzymes.

1 min read
Treatment

Does weight-loss surgery help the liver?

Yes. For people with obesity who qualify for surgery, it can clear MASH and improve scarring.

1 min read

Risks and follow-up 3

Risks and follow-up

Can fatty liver turn into cirrhosis or cancer?

It can, in a small share of people, usually over many years. The risk is mainly in people who already have advanced scarring.

1 min read
Risks and follow-up

Why does my heart matter if I have fatty liver?

Because heart disease, not liver disease, is the most common cause of death in people with fatty liver.

1 min read
Risks and follow-up

How often should I be checked?

It depends on your scarring stage. Low-risk people are usually re-checked every 1 to 3 years; people with more scarring are seen more often.

1 min read

Special situations 3

Special situations

I'm not overweight. Can I still have fatty liver?

Yes. This is called lean MASLD. It is linked to belly fat, insulin resistance and genes, and it needs the same checks.

1 min read
Special situations

Can children get fatty liver?

Yes, especially children with obesity. It needs care from a paediatric specialist and the whole family's help.

1 min read
Special situations

What about fatty liver and pregnancy?

Fatty liver raises the risk of diabetes and high blood pressure in pregnancy. Plan ahead with your doctor, ideally before you conceive.

1 min read
