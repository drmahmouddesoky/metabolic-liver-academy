Partners and independence

We welcome partners who share our mission. Our education stays independent: partners never write, review or approve what we publish.

Our partners

The Academy has no partners or sponsors at present and receives no funding. When that changes, each partner will be listed here by name, with exactly what it supports.

How partners can help

Societies and universities

Joint teaching modules, accredited learning, reviewers for clinical pages, and shared lecture series.

Patient groups

Help us choose the questions that matter, test new pages with patients, and share trusted information.

Industry and pharma

Unrestricted educational grants for a module, a lecture series, Meet the Expert or an awareness campaign.

Independence and conflict of interest

  • Partners and sponsors never write, review, approve or see our content before it is published.
  • Any support is unrestricted, and the supporter is named on what it supports.
  • Patient pages describe medicines by type, not brand, and never promote a product.
  • Everyone who writes or reviews content discloses relevant financial relationships. A person with a conflict does not review content about that company's products.
  • We do not share visitor data with anyone. We collect no personal data to share.
  • We do not display advertising.
