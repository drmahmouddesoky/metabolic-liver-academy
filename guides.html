The liver-friendly plate

Build lunch and dinner like this. No food is banned; the size of each part is what matters.

Half: vegetables and salad
Quarter: proteinfish, chicken, beans, lentils
Quarter: whole grainsbrown rice, bulgur, brown bread

More often

  • Olive oil instead of ghee or butter
  • Fish two times a week
  • A handful of unsalted nuts
  • Water, plain coffee, tea without sugar

Less often

  • Soft drinks, energy drinks and packaged juices
  • Sweets, pastries and white bread
  • Fried food and fast food
  • Big rice portions; dates in large numbers

Healthy shopping list

What goes into the trolley ends up on the plate.

Fresh

  • Leafy greens, cucumber, tomato, peppers
  • Whole fruit (not juice)
  • Fish, skinless chicken, eggs
  • Plain yoghurt, laban

Cupboard

  • Lentils, chickpeas, beans
  • Oats, bulgur, brown rice, freekeh
  • Extra-virgin olive oil
  • Unsalted nuts and seeds

Read the label

  • Sugar near the top of the ingredients list: put it back
  • Watch for glucose-fructose syrup
  • A long list of additives often means ultra-processed

8-week walking plan

Start where you are and build up to 150 minutes a week. Brisk means you can talk but not sing. In the hot months, walk early morning, late evening, or in a mall.

Fatty liver and fasting in Ramadan

Most people with fatty liver can fast safely, and Ramadan can even help weight loss if meals are planned.

Before Ramadan

  • See your doctor if you have diabetes, cirrhosis, or take insulin or several medicines. Doses and timing may need to change.
  • People with advanced cirrhosis may be advised not to fast.

At iftar

  • Break the fast with water and 1–3 dates, then pause.
  • Soup and salad first, then a plate built like the liver-friendly plate.
  • Keep fried samosas and sweets like kunafa and qatayef for small portions, not every night.

At suhoor

  • Eat it late. Choose slow foods: oats, beans, eggs, yoghurt, whole bread.
  • Drink water between iftar and suhoor, not sugary drinks such as Vimto or tamarind juice.

Stay active

  • Walk 20–30 minutes about an hour after iftar, or walk to taraweeh prayers.
  • Stop fasting and seek help for dizziness, confusion, very low or very high blood sugar, or vomiting.
