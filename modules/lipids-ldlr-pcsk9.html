Mastering Hyperlipidemia Management · Module 4 of 12

The gatekeeper: the LDL receptor and PCSK9

How the liver clears LDL, how PCSK9 destroys the receptor, what human genetics taught us, and how antibodies, siRNA and the first oral PCSK9 inhibitor use that knowledge.

Learning objectives

After this module you should be able to:

  1. Describe the LDL receptor cycle and the cell's response to the cholesterol it delivers.
  2. Explain how PCSK9 sends the receptor to the lysosome and why statins raise PCSK9.
  3. Relate gain- and loss-of-function variants in LDLR, APOB and PCSK9 to LDL-C and coronary risk.
  4. Compare monoclonal antibodies, inclisiran and enlicitide by mechanism, dosing, effect size and evidence.
  5. Explain why LDL-receptor–independent drugs are needed in homozygous familial hypercholesterolaemia.
  6. Apply the Saudi Heart Association position on early PCSK9-targeted therapy.
