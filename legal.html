Medical disclaimer

Everything on this site is for general education only. It is not medical advice, and it is not a diagnosis or a treatment plan. It does not create a doctor–patient relationship.

The risk checklist and the FIB-4 calculator are education tools. Their results can be wrong for some people. Always discuss your results and any decision about your health with your own doctor. Do not stop, start or change any medicine because of this site.

In an emergency, call your local emergency number or go to the nearest emergency department.

We try to keep the content accurate and up to date, but we cannot guarantee it. The Metabolic Liver Academy and its contributors accept no liability for any loss or harm that comes from relying on this site.

Privacy

  • The tools run only inside your own browser. What you type is not saved and not sent to us or anyone else.
  • We have no accounts, no forms that collect your data, no advertising and no tracking.
  • Your browser remembers your language choice (English or Arabic) on your own device. You can clear it at any time in your browser settings.
  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads fonts from Google Fonts and video thumbnails from YouTube. These services may see your IP address, as with any website. Their own privacy policies apply.
  • If you email us, we use your email only to reply.

Editorial policy: how we review content

  • Each page shows when it was last reviewed, and is reviewed at least once a year.
  • News posts are written in our own words and link to the original source. We do not copy articles.
