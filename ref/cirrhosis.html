MASLD cirrhosis: key care

Find portal hypertension, screen for liver cancer, and keep treating metabolic risk.

For healthcare professionals

Key points

  • Baveno VII "rule of five" for liver stiffness: 10, 15, 20, 25 kPa.
  • Liver cancer surveillance: ultrasound (± AFP) every 6 months in cirrhosis.
  • Carry on statins, diabetes care and weight management in compensated disease.
  • Approved MASH drugs are not yet licensed for cirrhosis.

Liver stiffness: the rule of five

LSM (VCTE)Meaning
<10 kPaCompensated advanced chronic liver disease (cACLD) unlikely
10–15 kPaSuggestive of cACLD
>15 kPaHighly suggestive of cACLD
≤15 kPa + platelets ≥150Rules out clinically significant portal hypertension (CSPH)
<20 kPa + platelets >150Screening endoscopy for varices can be skipped
≥25 kPaRules in CSPH (less reliable in obese MASLD)

Other essentials

  • Non-selective beta-blocker (carvedilol preferred) if CSPH, to prevent decompensation.
  • Vaccinate: hepatitis A and B, pneumococcus, influenza.
  • Watch for sarcopenia; avoid long fasts and give enough protein.
  • Refer for transplant assessment after decompensation or a MELD score of about 15 or more.

Advanced fibrosis without cirrhosis (F3)

Liver cancer can occur in MASLD before cirrhosis, but routine surveillance in F3 is not generally advised. Decide case by case.
