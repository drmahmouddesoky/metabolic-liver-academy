Non-invasive tests at a glance

Formulas, cut-offs and pitfalls for the tests used in MASLD.

For healthcare professionals

Key points

  • Blood scores (FIB-4, NFS) are best at ruling out advanced fibrosis.
  • VCTE (FibroScan) and ELF are the usual second-line tests.
  • MRE is the most accurate imaging test for fibrosis but costly and less available.
  • APRI was built for hepatitis C. It is not advised for MASLD.

Blood-based scores

TestInputsRule outRule inNotes
FIB-4Age, AST, ALT, platelets<1.3 (<2.0 if ≥65)≥2.67Free, first-line. Not for under 35.
NFSAge, BMI, diabetes/IFG, AST/ALT, platelets, albumin<−1.455 (<0.12 if ≥65)>0.676Similar accuracy to FIB-4. BMI and diabetes push it up.
APRIAST, platelets——Low accuracy in MASLD. Use FIB-4 instead.
ELFHA, PIIINP, TIMP-1 (lab test)<7.7≥9.8Second-line. Raised by other fibrotic conditions and age.

Formulas

  • FIB-4 = (age × AST) ÷ (platelets × √ALT)
  • NFS = −1.675 + 0.037 × age + 0.094 × BMI + 1.13 × (IFG or diabetes: 1, else 0) + 0.99 × AST/ALT − 0.013 × platelets − 0.66 × albumin (g/dL)
  • APRI = (AST ÷ upper limit of normal) ÷ platelets × 100

Things that falsely raise liver stiffness

  • Recent meal (fast 3 hours).
  • High ALT or acute hepatitis.
  • Heart failure or liver congestion.
  • Biliary obstruction.
  • Heavy recent alcohol intake.
