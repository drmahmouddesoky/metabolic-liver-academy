Definitions and diagnosis of MASLD

Steatosis plus at least one cardiometabolic risk factor, with no other cause, is MASLD.

For healthcare professionals

Key points

  • MASLD = hepatic steatosis (imaging, biopsy or CAP) + ≥1 of 5 cardiometabolic criteria.
  • MASH is the inflammatory form. It is still a histological diagnosis.
  • MetALD = MASLD plus moderate alcohol intake (140–350 g/week women, 210–420 g/week men).
  • Always look for other causes before labelling MASLD.

The five cardiometabolic criteria (adults)

One is enough. Each criterion also counts if the patient is on treatment for it.

CriterionThreshold
WeightBMI ≥25 kg/m² (≥23 in Asian people), or waist >94 cm men / >80 cm women
GlucoseFasting glucose ≥5.6 mmol/L (100 mg/dL), 2-h glucose ≥7.8 mmol/L (140 mg/dL), HbA1c ≥5.7%, or type 2 diabetes
Blood pressure≥130/85 mmHg
Triglycerides≥1.70 mmol/L (150 mg/dL)
HDL cholesterol≤1.0 mmol/L (40 mg/dL) men, ≤1.3 mmol/L (50 mg/dL) women

The steatotic liver disease family

TermMeaning
SLDSteatotic liver disease: the umbrella term for any fatty liver
MASLDSteatosis + ≥1 cardiometabolic criterion, little or no alcohol

Cryptogenic SLDSteatosis with no cardiometabolic criterion and no other cause

Rule out other causes

A standard first work-up for raised liver tests or steatosis:

  • Alcohol history in grams per week; AUDIT-C helps.
  • HBsAg and anti-HCV.
  • Medicines: amiodarone, methotrexate, tamoxifen, corticosteroids, valproate, some antiretrovirals.
  • Ferritin and transferrin saturation. A high ferritin is common in MASLD; check saturation before thinking of haemochromatosis.
  • If ALT is high or the picture is unusual: autoimmune markers and IgG, ceruloplasmin (under 40 years), alpha-1 antitrypsin, coeliac serology, TSH.

When is a biopsy needed?

Rarely, now. Consider it when non-invasive tests disagree, when another liver disease is suspected, or for a trial. Biopsy is the only way to confirm MASH and grade activity.
