FIB-4 calculator

For healthcare professionals. The first step of the fibrosis pathway in adults with MASLD.

Enter the patient's values

FIB-4 = (age × AST) ÷ (platelets × √ALT). Below 1.3 (2.0 if aged 65 or over) makes advanced fibrosis unlikely; 2.67 or above makes it likely. Not validated under age 35. Based on the EASL–EASD–EASO 2024 and AASLD 2023 guidance.
