Which test, when?

  1. Start with FIB-4 (or NFS) for every adult with fatty liver or metabolic risk.
  2. If it is not low, do a FibroScan. Use the explainer, then Agile 3+ and Agile 4 to sharpen the answer.
  3. FAST helps find active MASH with significant fibrosis, for example before referral for treatment.
  4. FLI and HSI screen for liver fat when no scan is available. They do not measure fibrosis.

Based on the EASL–EASD–EASO 2024 and AASLD 2023 guidance. See the fibrosis pathway. Why no APRI? It was built for hepatitis C and is less accurate in MASLD.
