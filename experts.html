Meet the Expert

Short interviews with respected liver, metabolic and obesity specialists from around the world and from the Middle East and North Africa, so patients and doctors hear from many trusted voices.

How we choose and publish interviews

  • Experts are invited for their clinical and research work in liver, metabolic and obesity medicine.
  • Every expert approves their bio, photo and interview in writing before anything is published.
  • Each interview has a full transcript, plain-language takeaways, and the expert's declared conflicts of interest.
  • Experts are not paid by sponsors to take part, and interviews never promote a product. See our independence policy.
  • Interviews are education, not personal medical advice.
