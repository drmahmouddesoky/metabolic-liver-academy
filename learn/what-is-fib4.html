What is the FIB-4 score?

In short

FIB-4 is a free score calculated from your age and three routine blood results. It tells your doctor whether serious scarring is unlikely or needs a closer look.

1 min read

It uses age, AST, ALT and platelet count. No extra test is needed if you already have these results.

A low score (below 1.3, or below 2.0 if you are 65 or older) means advanced scarring is unlikely. A higher score means the next step is a liver stiffness scan such as FibroScan.

FIB-4 is less reliable under age 35. It is a guide for your doctor, not a diagnosis.
