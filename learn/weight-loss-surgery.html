Does weight-loss surgery help the liver?

In short

Yes. For people with obesity who qualify for surgery, it can clear MASH and improve scarring.

Your liver should be checked before surgery. If there is cirrhosis, surgery is riskier and should be done in a specialist centre.

Endoscopic options, such as a gastric balloon or endoscopic sleeve, are less invasive. Their liver benefits look promising but are less proven.
