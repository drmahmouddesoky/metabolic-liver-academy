Why does my heart matter if I have fatty liver?

In short

Because heart disease, not liver disease, is the most common cause of death in people with fatty liver.

Fatty liver and heart disease share the same roots. Looking after one helps the other.

  • Know your blood pressure, sugar (HbA1c) and cholesterol numbers
  • Take your heart and diabetes medicines as prescribed
  • Stop smoking
  • Move more every day
