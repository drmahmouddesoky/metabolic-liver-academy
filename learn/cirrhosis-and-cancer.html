Can fatty liver turn into cirrhosis or cancer?

In short

It can, in a small share of people, usually over many years. The risk is mainly in people who already have advanced scarring.

1 min read

Cirrhosis means the liver is badly scarred. It can lead to fluid in the belly, bleeding, confusion and liver cancer.

People with cirrhosis need a liver ultrasound about every 6 months to find cancer early, when it is easier to treat.

Treating the cause early, by losing weight, controlling diabetes and avoiding alcohol, lowers the risk.
