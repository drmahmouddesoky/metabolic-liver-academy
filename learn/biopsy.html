Do I need a liver biopsy?

In short

Usually not. Today most people are assessed with blood-test scores and scans. A biopsy is kept for unclear cases.

A biopsy takes a tiny piece of liver with a needle. It gives detailed information but carries small risks such as pain or bleeding.

Your specialist may suggest one if test results disagree, if another liver disease is suspected, or for a clinical trial.
