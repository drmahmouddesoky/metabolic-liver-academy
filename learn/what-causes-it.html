What causes fatty liver?

In short

Mostly insulin resistance: the body struggles to handle sugar and fat, so the liver stores the extra as fat.

Your doctor will also check for other causes, such as alcohol, some medicines, or viral hepatitis, because they need different care.

  • Extra weight, especially around the waist
  • Type 2 diabetes or prediabetes
  • High blood pressure
  • High triglycerides or low HDL (good) cholesterol
  • Too many sugary drinks and processed foods
  • Little physical activity
  • Genes: fatty liver can run in families
