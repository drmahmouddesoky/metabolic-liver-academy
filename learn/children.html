Can children get fatty liver?

In short

Yes, especially children with obesity. It needs care from a paediatric specialist and the whole family's help.

1 min read

Doctors suggest a simple ALT blood test from about age 10 for children with obesity, or with overweight plus other risks such as diabetes in the family.

Children's tests are different: adult scores like FIB-4 are not used for them.

Family changes work best: fewer sugary drinks at home, more active play, less screen time, and regular family meals.
