Does fatty liver cause symptoms?

In short

Usually not. Many people feel completely well, even when there is scarring.

1 min read

Some people notice tiredness or a dull ache on the right side under the ribs.

Clear symptoms usually appear only in advanced cirrhosis. That is why people at risk should be checked, not wait for symptoms.

  • Get urgent care for: vomiting blood, black stools, yellow eyes, confusion, or a fast-swelling belly.
