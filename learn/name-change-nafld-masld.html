Why did the name change from NAFLD to MASLD?

In short

In 2023, liver societies changed the name to describe the real cause, metabolic problems, and to remove words that felt judgemental.

1 min read

The old name, NAFLD (non-alcoholic fatty liver disease), described the disease by what it is not. Many patients also found the words "alcoholic" and "fatty" stigmatising.

The new umbrella name is steatotic liver disease (SLD). Under it sit MASLD (metabolic), MetALD (metabolic plus moderate alcohol), and ALD (alcohol-related).

Almost everyone who had NAFLD now has MASLD. Research done under the old name still applies.
