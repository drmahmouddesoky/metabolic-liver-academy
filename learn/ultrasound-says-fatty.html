My ultrasound says "fatty liver". What now?

In short

Don't panic and don't ignore it. The next step is to find out if there is scarring, starting with a simple blood-test score called FIB-4.

An ultrasound shows fat but cannot measure scarring well. That is why the next tests matter more than the word "fatty" on the report.

  • Ask your doctor to calculate your FIB-4 score
  • Check blood sugar, blood pressure and cholesterol
  • Tell your doctor how much alcohol you drink, honestly
  • Start small lifestyle changes now
