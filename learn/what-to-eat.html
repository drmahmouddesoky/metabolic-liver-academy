What should I eat?

In short

A Mediterranean-style diet works best: lots of vegetables, legumes, whole grains, fish and olive oil, with fewer sugary and processed foods.

1 min read

No single "liver diet" or special food cures fatty liver. The overall pattern, kept up for years, is what counts.

  • Fill half your plate with vegetables
  • Choose brown bread, bulgur or brown rice over white
  • Eat fish twice a week; beans and lentils often
  • Use olive oil instead of ghee or butter
  • Cut sugary drinks, sweets and fried food
