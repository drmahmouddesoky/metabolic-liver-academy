Is fatty liver serious?

In short

For most people it stays mild. But in some it leads to scarring, cirrhosis or liver cancer, and it raises the risk of heart disease in everyone.

1 min read

The most important sign of how serious it is, is the amount of scarring (fibrosis). Little or no scarring means a low risk of liver problems.

The good news: fat, inflammation and even early scarring can improve when you lose weight and treat diabetes and blood pressure.
