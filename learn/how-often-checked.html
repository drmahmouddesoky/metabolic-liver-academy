How often should I be checked?

In short

It depends on your scarring stage. Low-risk people are usually re-checked every 1 to 3 years; people with more scarring are seen more often.

Keep a copy of your results so you can see the trend over time.

  • Low FIB-4: repeat FIB-4 every 1–3 years with your family doctor
  • Higher scores: follow-up with a liver specialist and repeat stiffness scans
  • Cirrhosis: ultrasound about every 6 months for cancer screening
