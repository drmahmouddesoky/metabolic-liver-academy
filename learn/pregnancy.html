What about fatty liver and pregnancy?

In short

Fatty liver raises the risk of diabetes and high blood pressure in pregnancy. Plan ahead with your doctor, ideally before you conceive.

1 min read

Losing weight before pregnancy is helpful. During pregnancy, the aim is healthy eating and safe activity, not weight loss.

The new MASH medicines are not used in pregnancy. Tell your doctor if you are pregnant or planning to be.
