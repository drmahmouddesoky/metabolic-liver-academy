What is MASH, and how is it different?

In short

MASH is the active, inflamed form of MASLD. The fat is now harming liver cells, and over years this can cause scarring.

Most people with MASLD have fat alone. In some, the fat triggers inflammation and cell injury. This is MASH: metabolic dysfunction-associated steatohepatitis (old name: NASH).

MASH matters because it can lead to fibrosis (scarring). Scarring is graded from F0 (none) to F4 (cirrhosis).

You cannot tell MASLD from MASH by symptoms. Blood-test scores and liver stiffness scans help doctors judge the risk.
