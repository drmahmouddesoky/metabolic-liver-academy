Do herbs, "detox" or supplements help?

In short

No herb, detox or supplement is proven to treat fatty liver. Some herbal products can even harm the liver.

1 min read

The liver cleans itself. "Liver cleanse" products are not needed and can waste money.

Concentrated herbal extracts and some body-building supplements have caused liver injury. Always tell your doctor about anything you take, including herbs.
