What exercise helps fatty liver?

In short

Any activity you can keep doing. Aim for 150 minutes a week of brisk walking or similar, plus some strength exercise.

Exercise lowers liver fat even if your weight does not change much.

Start small, for example 10 minutes a day, and build up. In hot weather, walk early, late, or indoors.

Add strength exercise twice a week, such as light weights, resistance bands or body-weight moves.
