Doctors' reference library

Short, practical summaries of the main MASLD and MASH guidelines, with cut-offs, tables and references. For healthcare professionals.

Diagnose and stage

  • Definitions and diagnosis of MASLD
    Steatosis plus at least one cardiometabolic risk factor, with no other cause, is MASLD.
  • Who to test for liver fibrosis
    Test the people most likely to have advanced fibrosis, not everyone.
  • The two-step fibrosis pathway
    FIB-4 first, then elastography or ELF if FIB-4 is not low.
  • Non-invasive tests at a glance
    Formulas, cut-offs and pitfalls for the tests used in MASLD.
  • FIB-4 calculator: method, users and limits
    How our FIB-4 calculator works, who it is for, and when not to rely on it.

Treat

  • Lifestyle treatment: the targets
    Weight loss is the base of treatment for every patient, at every stage.
  • Drug treatment for MASH
    Two drugs are approved for non-cirrhotic MASH with F2–F3 fibrosis: resmetirom and semaglutide.
  • Heart and metabolic care in MASLD
    Heart disease, not liver disease, is the top cause of death in most MASLD patients.
  • Metabolic and bariatric surgery
    Surgery gives the largest, longest-lasting improvement in MASH, in the right patient.

Follow up and special groups

  • Follow-up and referral
    How often to retest, and when to send the patient to a liver specialist.
  • MASLD cirrhosis: key care
    Find portal hypertension, screen for liver cancer, and keep treating metabolic risk.
  • Special groups
    Children, lean patients, older adults, young adults and people who drink alcohol.
