Fatty liver is common, silent, and treatable when found early.

Clear, trusted education on MASLD and MASH in Arabic and English, for patients, families, and the doctors who care for them.

I'm a patient Patient Portal, for patients and families: plain guides, 28 questions answered, and a risk check. I'm a doctor Physician Portal, for healthcare professionals: a reference library, FIB-4 and NFS calculators, and learning by role.

Over years, fatty liver can scar the liver. Doctors grade this scarring (fibrosis) in five stages:

F0No scarring
F1Mild
F2Moderate
F3Advanced
F4Cirrhosis

About the Academy

The Metabolic Liver Academy is an independent, bilingual education academy for fatty liver disease. Everything we publish is free, follows international and Saudi guidelines, lists its sources, and carries a public review record.

  • Founded and directed by Dr. Mahmoud Al-Desoky, Consultant Internist and Gastroenterologist
  • Open to specialist reviewers and faculty from across the region
  • No sponsors, no advertising, no tracking
