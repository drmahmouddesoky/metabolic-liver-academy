F0No scarring
F1Mild
F2Moderate
F3Advanced
F4Cirrhosis

About the Academy

The Metabolic Liver Academy is an independent, bilingual education academy for fatty liver disease. Everything we publish is free, follows international and Saudi guidelines, lists its sources, and carries a public review record.

  • Founded and directed by Dr. Mahmoud Desoky, Consultant Internist and Gastroenterologist
  • Open to specialist reviewers and faculty from across the region
  • No sponsors, no advertising, no tracking
